# G01 - Welcome and Quick Start

For: {{owner_name}} at {{business_name}}

Your workspace is at **{{dashboard_url}}**. Calls forwarded to your AI assistant become leads, follow-up tasks and a customer history your team can work from.

## Your first week
1. **Sign in.** Open {{dashboard_url}}. Enter your work email and select **Send Sign-In Code**. Type the one-time code from your email and select **Sign In**. To leave, use **Sign out** in the top bar (or open {{dashboard_url}}/logout).
2. **Look around.** The top bar lists your pages. Start with **Lead Management**, **Customer 360**, **Voicemails** and **Agent Workspace** (see G03).
3. **Forward your calls.** Follow G02. We recommend starting with after-hours or no-answer forwarding only for the first week.
4. **Place a test call** from another phone to {{business_name}}'s main line. In **Agent Workspace**, select **Refresh Interactions** to see it.
5. **Meet your team.** Ask us to add teammates (G11).
6. **Review daily** for the first week: new leads, voicemails, and any call that went to the wrong place. Send corrections to {{support_email}} (G12).

## Good to know
- Pages update only when you press their Refresh button (for example **Refresh Leads**). They do not refresh by themselves.
- The AI assistant is not an emergency service. Never route 911 or emergency lines through it.

**If something looks wrong:** see G13, or email {{support_email}} with what you saw and the time.
