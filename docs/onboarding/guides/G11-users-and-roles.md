# G11 - Users and Roles

For: {{owner_name}}

## Signing in
Everyone signs in at {{dashboard_url}} with their work email: enter the email, select **Send Sign-In Code**, type the **One-Time Code**, select **Sign In**. There are no passwords. Only people we have added can sign in. To leave, select **Sign out** in the top bar.

## Adding or removing people
There is no user-management screen in the app. Email {{support_email}} with the person's name, email and role, and we add or remove them and confirm.

## Roles
| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including closing leads |
| Admin | Everything, including closing leads |
| Supervisor | Assign and edit leads, close leads |
| Operator | Edit open leads |
| Agent | Work leads assigned to them (no commercial fields) |
| Viewer | Read-only |

The role shows at the top right of Lead Management.

## Good practice
- Give people the lowest role that lets them do their job.
- Tell us the same day someone leaves.
- Do not share sign-in codes.

**If something looks wrong:** if someone cannot sign in, check the email address spelling and spam folder for the code, then email {{support_email}}.
