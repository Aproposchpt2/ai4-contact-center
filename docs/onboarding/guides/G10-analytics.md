# G10 - Analytics

For: the owner at {{business_name}}. Page: **Voice Operations & Analytics** at {{dashboard_url}}/voice-operations.

## Load the numbers
1. Open the page and select **Refresh Voice Analytics**. It loads a snapshot; it does not update by itself.
2. Read the sections:
   - **Call handling / Runtime distribution**
   - **Intent mix** - "Why callers are reaching us"
   - **Queue load** - "Where calls are routed"
   - **Quality** and **Compliance** - operational quality signals and open findings
   - **Daily volume** - call activity by day
   - **Agent workload** - assigned voice interactions
   - **Recent calls** - each links to the call in Agent Workspace

## A weekly routine (first month)
1. Refresh and look at Daily volume and Intent mix.
2. Open a few Recent calls and read the transcripts (G09).
3. Note any call that went to the wrong queue and send it to {{support_email}}.

## Where to find it
**Voice Operations** is in the top bar. (The separate "Analytics Engine" page, a tool for uploading your own JSON data, is not part of a standard customer workspace.)

## Note
These figures are operational signals from your calls, not guarantees or forecasts of results.

**If something looks wrong:** if the page is empty, you may have no calls yet in the period: place a test call and refresh. If numbers look wrong, email {{support_email}} with what you expected.
