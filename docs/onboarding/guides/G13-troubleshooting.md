# G13 - Troubleshooting

For: everyone at {{business_name}}. Workspace: {{dashboard_url}}. Support: {{support_email}}.

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| **The AI did not answer** | Confirm forwarding is on for that number (G02b). Call from another phone. If a person's phone rang, forwarding is not active or the no-answer time is too long. |
| **A lead is missing** | On Lead Management press **Refresh Leads**, and clear the search and filters (stage, priority, ownership). A call ends a lead only after the AI has a name, a way to reach them and the reason for the call. |
| **A call is missing in Agent Workspace** | Press **Refresh Interactions** and check the ALL filter. Then check the time zone of the call. |
| **A voicemail is missing** | Press **Check Voicemail**. |
| **Call went to the wrong department** | Note the caller's number and time and email us (G12). Routing is changed by us. |
| **Can't sign in** | Check the email spelling and spam folder for the code. Codes are single-use; request a new one with **Send Sign-In Code**. If it still fails, email us. |
| **Bounced back to the sign-in page** | Your session ended. Sign in again. |
| **A page I need is missing from the top bar** | It may not be in your plan. Ask us. |
| **"You do not have access to this workspace"** | You are signed in with an email not added to this workspace, or on the wrong address. Sign out, then sign in at {{dashboard_url}}. |
| **A button is greyed out** | Your role may be read-only or the lead is not assigned to you (G04, G11). |
| **Wrong caller number on a lead** | Some carriers change the number when forwarding (G02b). Tell us. |
| **The AI said something wrong** | G09: report it with the time and what it said. |

## Stop the AI right now
Turn call forwarding off with the undo step for your carrier (G02b). Calls ring your team again immediately.

## Emergencies
Do not rely on the AI assistant for emergencies. Call 911.

**If something looks wrong:** email {{support_email}} with what you saw, the page, and the time.
