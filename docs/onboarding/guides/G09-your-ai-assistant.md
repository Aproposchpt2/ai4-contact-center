# G09 - Your AI Assistant

For: everyone at {{business_name}}

## What it does
When a call is forwarded, the AI assistant answers as {{business_name}}, says it is an AI assistant, finds out why the person is calling, collects the details your team needs, and hands urgent calls to a person where you have set that up. The result becomes a lead in Lead Management.

## What it will not do
It will not quote prices unless {{business_name}} approved a price list, promise arrival times or outcomes, give legal, medical or financial advice, take card numbers by phone, or answer questions outside the approved answers. When unsure it says a team member will follow up.

## See what it said
1. Open **Agent Workspace** and select **Refresh Interactions**.
2. Select a call. Read the **Transcript**, and the **AI Assist** panel (detected reason, suggested replies). Other panels show **QA** and **Compliance Findings**.
3. Use the **VOICE** filter to see only phone calls.

## Report a bad call
Email {{support_email}} with the caller's number, the time, what the AI said, and what it should have said. We tune the script and re-test before changing it.

## Emergencies
The AI assistant is **not** a 911 or emergency service. If your business has emergencies, tell us how they should be handled; we set a direct transfer. Never depend on the AI alone.

**If something looks wrong:** if the greeting or wording is off, or it says something wrong about your business, email {{support_email}} right away.
