# G06 - Customer 360

For: managers at {{business_name}}. Page: **Customer 360**.

One screen with everything about a caller: their calls, texts and chats, their leads, open tasks and past activity.

## Find a customer
1. Open **Customer 360** and select **Refresh Customers**.
2. Use the search box (name, company, email, phone, tags, source or priority). The count shows "N of N contacts".
3. Select a contact in the **Contacts** list on the left.

## What you see
- **Header:** name, company or email, priority, score, and a **DO NOT CONTACT** badge if set.
- **Summary tiles:** Interactions, Open Leads, Open Tasks, Pipeline Value, Weighted Value, Last Interaction.
- **Details:** Email, Phone, Preferred Channel, Lead Source, SMS Consent, Email Consent, Contactable, Tags.
- **Channel counts:** Voice, SMS, Web Chat.
- **Lead Portfolio:** each lead with stage, value and next action. Use **Lead Lifecycle** or **Activity + Tasks** to jump to it.
- **Cross-Channel Interaction History:** each call/text/chat with time, queue, agent and the detected reason for the call.
- **Open Work:** tasks linked to this contact (**Open Lead Operations**).
- **Customer Activity Timeline:** notes and events (**Open Lead** where linked).

## Tips
- Check **Contactable** and the consent fields before reaching out.
- The list and detail update only when you press **Refresh Customers**.

**If something looks wrong:** if a caller is missing, refresh and search by phone number. If a caller appears twice (different numbers or spellings), tell {{support_email}} so they can be reviewed.
