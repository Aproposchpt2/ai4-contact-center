# G05 - Tasks and Activities

For: agents at {{business_name}}. Page: **Lead Activity + Task Operations** (open it with **Activity + Tasks** on a lead in Lead Management).

## Record an Activity (what happened)
1. Open the lead, then **Activity + Tasks**.
2. Under **Record Activity**, fill in **Type** (default "note"), **Direction** (internal, inbound or outbound), **Subject**, **Notes** and **Outcome**.
3. Select **Record Activity**. It appears at the top of **Activity Timeline** (newest first).

## Create a Task (what must happen next)
1. Under **Create Task**, enter **Title**, **Description**, **Type**, **Priority**, **Due** and **Assigned Agent** (or leave Unassigned).
2. Select **Create Task**.
3. Tasks appear under **Lead Tasks** with their status, due date and agent.

## Update a Task
Change a task's status in **Lead Tasks**: pending, in progress, completed or cancelled. Tasks past their due date and not completed or cancelled are counted as overdue at the top of the page.

## Tips
- Log a note after every customer contact so the next person has context.
- Use **Lead Lifecycle** to go back to the lead.
- Your role decides what you may change; read-only users cannot record activities or tasks.

**If something looks wrong:** if a button is greyed out you may not be assigned to the lead or your role is read-only. Ask a manager or {{support_email}}.
