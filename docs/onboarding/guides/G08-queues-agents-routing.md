# G08 - Queues, Agents and Routing

For: the owner or manager at {{business_name}}.

## The idea
A **queue** is a team or type of work (for example Scheduling, Billing, Emergency). The AI assistant works out why someone is calling and routes the call to the right queue. **Agents** are your people; each can be linked to queues.

## Where you can see it
- **Agent Workspace:** every call in **Recent Interactions** shows its **queue**, agent (if assigned), status and the reason for the call. Use the **ALL / VOICE / SMS / WEB CHAT** filters.
- **Voice Operations & Analytics** (G10): **Queue load** ("Where calls are routed") and **Agent workload**.

## Work an interaction (Agent Workspace)
1. Select **Refresh Interactions**, then select a call.
2. In **Interaction Operations**: **Claim Interaction** (take it), **Callback Pending**, **Complete + Disposition**, or **Reopen Interaction**.
3. When completing, choose a **Disposition** (Resolved, Callback Required, Follow Up, Transferred, No Action Required) and add an optional note.

## Changing routing
Your queues, business hours, holidays and transfer numbers were set up from your intake form. To change them (new team, new number, new hours), send a change request (G12). We make the change, test it and confirm with you. You cannot edit routing in the app yourself.

**If something looks wrong:** if a call went to the wrong team, send {{support_email}} the caller's number, the time, and where it should have gone.
