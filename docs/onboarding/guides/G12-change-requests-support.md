# G12 - Change Requests and Support

For: {{owner_name}}

## Ask for a change
Email **{{support_email}}** for any of these:
- New or changed greeting, answers, or wording the AI uses
- Changes to routing: teams, transfer numbers, hours, holidays
- Adding or removing users, or changing a role
- New questions the AI should be able to answer

Include: what to change, when, and who approved it. For hours or transfer numbers, give the exact numbers and times.

## What happens next
1. We confirm we understood the request.
2. We prepare the change on a draft, test it with practice calls, and show you results.
3. We deploy the tested version. Earlier versions are kept, so we can roll back if needed.
4. We confirm when it is live. Please make a test call.

Script and routing changes go through us on purpose: they keep your calls predictable and let us undo mistakes.

## Urgent problems
If callers are being sent somewhere wrong right now: **turn call forwarding off** (G02b), then email {{support_email}}.

## Review meetings
We check in on days 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30, then monthly. Bring bad calls and questions.

**If something looks wrong:** if you do not hear back within the response time in your agreement, reply to your original email and add "URGENT" to the subject.
