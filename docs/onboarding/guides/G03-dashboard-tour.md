# G03 - Dashboard Tour

For: everyone at {{business_name}}

## The top bar
The bar across the top shows your workspace name and the pages turned on for you (for example **Lead Management**, **Customer 360**, **Voicemails**, **Agent Workspace**, **Analytics Engine**, **Dashboard**). It also has a **Light / Black** color switch and a **Sign out** button. Your team sees only the pages included in your plan.

## Refresh model
Pages load when you open them and update **only when you press their Refresh button**, for example **Refresh Leads**, **Refresh Customers**, **Check Voicemail**, **Refresh Interactions**. Nothing updates in the background. Each page shows "Last refreshed" so you know how fresh it is.

## The Dashboard page
**Dashboard** opens **Saved Flows**: the call flows saved for {{business_name}}, with your workspace name and role shown under the title. From here you can open a flow or use **Agent Workspace ->**. Flow changes are handled by your Apropos contact (see G12); you do not need to change flows day to day.

## Where things live
| I want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| Work new leads | Lead Management (G04, G05) |
| See everything about a caller | Customer 360 (G06) |
| Listen to messages | Voicemails (G07) |
| See calls and what the AI said | Agent Workspace (G09) |
| See call volume and reasons for calls | Voice Operations & Analytics (G10) |

## Signing out
Select **Sign out** in the top bar. You return to the sign-in page. Do this on shared computers.

**If something looks wrong:** a page missing from the top bar may not be included in your plan. Ask {{support_email}}.
