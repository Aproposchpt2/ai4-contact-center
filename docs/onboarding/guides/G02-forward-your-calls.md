# G02 - Forward Your Calls

For: whoever controls {{business_name}}'s phone line. Carrier-specific steps are in **G02b**.

You keep your number. Your phone system forwards calls to **{{forwarding_number}}**, where the AI assistant answers.

## Pick a forwarding style
| Style | What happens | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| After-hours only | Calls forward outside {{hours}} | Safest first week |
| No answer / busy | Your team gets the first chance; the AI catches missed calls | Most businesses |
| All calls | The AI answers everything | Only after go-live sign-off |

## Steps
1. Choose a style with your Apropos onboarding contact.
2. Set forwarding using G02b for your carrier or phone system. For no-answer forwarding, set it to roughly 4 rings so a person gets the first chance.
3. **Test:** call your main line from a different phone and let it forward. Confirm the AI answers with {{business_name}}'s greeting.
4. In **Agent Workspace**, select **Refresh Interactions** and confirm the call appears.
5. **Know how to undo it** (G02b). Turning forwarding off is instant and needs no help from us.

## Important
- **Never forward 911 or any emergency line** to the AI.
- Some carriers replace the caller's number when forwarding. If names or numbers look wrong on leads, tell us.

**If something looks wrong:** turn forwarding off first, then email {{support_email}} with the time of your test call.
